The frame gate

Both sides must hold the same primer bytes, identified by fingerprint df2f8b9972c40bfdd132366a58b4780f65f23feba1ef20a060c25d4eff2661ba. Show a different primer and the library refuses the comparison in its own words:

different primer
different primer: the two geometries are incommensurable; only exact identity can be compared

So the gate answers three ways rather than one: same_primer_comparable: true, different_primer_comparable: false, primers_differ: true. The transmitted seed is 3,300 bytes against 67,561 bytes reconstructed, a factor of 20.47.

What the checks refused to hide disagreement

The pre-registration expected kitchen_alone strictly nearest to kitchen_with_friend and then shared_workshop. Measured: both at Hamming 9, so strict_ordering_holds: false while the weak ordering near ≤ mid < far < furthest holds. The test was kept rather than retuned.

Where this stops

Nothing here is a claim about meaning: the address reports what an agent said and never what it understood. There is no zero-knowledge proof of correct derivation, because the root commits to a set of statements rather than to the reasoning that selected them.

Locality leaks neighbourhood information: two addresses 9 syllables apart reveal that the places are near, which the root deliberately does not. The evidence rests on a six-place population and a shared vocabulary; the 204-place population is used for calibration, while the headline is 6 of 6, and every family was handed the same 45 statements, so the 88.8% statement agreement is inherited rather than derived.

Every number here came from parties with no reason to lie, and none of them was tested against one that had a reason, so the checks measure reproducibility and not resistance to a false answer.
